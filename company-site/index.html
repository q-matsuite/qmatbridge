q-matsuite
Open-source research software

Materials data, ready for quantum simulation.

q-matsuite builds open tools that connect classical materials databases to fault-tolerant quantum algorithms, with provenance and reproducibility built in rather than bolted on.

Projects

QMatBridge early stage

A Python library that turns materials-database records into a neutral, hashable description of a first-quantized Hamiltonian, for benchmarking block-encodings and qubitization methods.

How we work

  • openCode under the MIT license; schemas change through public discussion.
  • provenanceEvery record says where it came from, so results can be compared and reproduced.
  • narrowEach tool does one job well and interoperates with the ecosystem rather than replacing it.
