# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers of QMatBridge, pledge to make
participation in our project and community a respectful, inclusive, and
harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
and scientifically rigorous community.

---

## Our Standards

**Behaviors that contribute to a positive environment:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints, scientific approaches, and levels of expertise
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback graciously
- Acknowledging prior work and citing contributors appropriately
- Focusing on what is best for the scientific community and the project
- Showing empathy toward other community members

**Unacceptable behaviors include:**

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Dismissing or belittling contributions based on the contributor's identity
  or career stage
- Derogatory comments, personal attacks, or political insults
- Publishing others' private information (e.g., institutional affiliation,
  email address) without explicit permission
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of others' scientific work
- Any conduct that would be considered unprofessional in an academic or
  research context

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull
requests, Discussions, the repository wiki, and any community event or
communication channel where individuals represent QMatBridge.

It also applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces,
such as conference presentations, posters, or social media posts that reference
QMatBridge.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers
at **robertomsreis@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly, treated
with strict confidentiality, and investigated in good faith.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and safety of the reporter.
Details of the specific incident will not be shared without consent.

**Enforcement actions, in increasing severity:**

1. **Correction** — A private written explanation of the behavior and a request
   to change it.
2. **Warning** — A formal warning with specific consequences for continued
   behavior. No interaction with involved parties for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban** — Temporary removal from all community interaction spaces.
4. **Permanent ban** — Permanent removal from all community spaces for severe,
   repeated, or egregious violations.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face repercussions as determined by other members of the project leadership.
